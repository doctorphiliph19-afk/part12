T h e   N e e d

★ Refugees in Kakuma face extraordinary challenges — conflict, loss, prolonged uncertainty, and stressful living conditions. These experiences take a heavy emotional and psychological toll, yet specialized counselors and creative outlets are scarce. Vulnerable individuals — especially children, youth, women, and trauma survivors — are left without safe, culturally relevant spaces to process emotions, rebuild confidence, and connect. ★

At Kakuma Creative Zone, we believe creative and community-driven support is essential. By integrating arts, expression, and psychosocial support into community programs, we help refugees heal, connect, and thrive.

About us

We use creativity to rebuild hope in one of the world's largest refugee camps.

For decades, Kakuma has been home to hundreds of thousands of people fleeing conflict. Inside its dust and resilience, an extraordinary creative culture has grown — songs in twelve languages, dances passed across borders, photographs that refuse to be forgotten.

Kakuma Creative Zone exists to give that culture a stage, a market, and a voice. We document artists, run workshops, connect them to paid opportunities, and bring their work to audiences across Kenya and the world.

What we do

Art, technology, dance, poetry, and culture — in the hands of refugee creators.

Kakuma Creative Zone is a community hub where art, technology, dance, poetry, and culture meet to empower creators in Kakuma Refugee Camp and Turkana County. We turn stories of resilience into creative action through:

🎨

Creative Workshops

Training youth in photography, spoken word, design, and digital storytelling.

🎤

Cultural Events

Hosting music showcases, exhibitions, and performances that celebrate identity and unity.

💻

Digital Skills Programs

Teaching coding, media production, and online collaboration to connect local talent globally.

🤝

Community Projects

Partnering with organizations to create art that inspires social change and inclusion.

🎸

Instrument Training

Teaching guitar, keyboard, drums, and traditional instruments with practical sessions.

🎧

Music Production

Guiding learners in recording, mixing, and digital sound design using accessible tools.

Our Work

Five program pillars guiding KCZ's work in Kakuma

Kakuma Creative Zone supports refugee and host-community creatives through safe, practical, and inclusive programs that turn talent into livelihood, healing and connection.

Creative Expression Skills & Livelihoods Cultural Identity

Pillar 01

Music, Performance & Healing

Vocal training, instrument access and recording sessions for emerging musicians from every block of the camp — using sound as a path to healing and income.

Programs under this pillar

  • Voice & Instrument Coaching
  • Studio Recording Sessions
  • Live Performance Bookings

Focus

  • Talent development
  • Mental health through sound
  • Paid gigs and royalties
  • Cross-camp collaboration

Pillar 02

Storytelling, Poetry & Journalism

Workshops in spoken word, journalism and creative writing so refugees can tell their own stories in their own words — and reach a global audience.

Programs under this pillar

  • Spoken Word Open Mics
  • Creative Writing Cohorts
  • Community Journalism Lab

Focus

  • Voice and agency
  • Authentic refugee narratives
  • Multilingual publishing
  • Truthful representation

Pillar 03

Visual Arts, Photography & Film

Cameras-in-hand mentorship, exhibitions, and visual documentation of camp life by the people who live it — building portfolios that open doors.

Programs under this pillar

  • Photography Mentorship
  • Mobile Filmmaking Lab
  • Community Exhibitions

Focus

  • Self-representation
  • Portfolio-grade output
  • Equipment access
  • Paid commissions
