Exercise 19: Connecting the frontend and backend

The frontend and backend communicate through Nginx.

- Nginx is exposed on port 8080.
- Frontend requests use VITE_BACKEND_URL=/api.
- Axios uses import.meta.env.VITE_BACKEND_URL.
- Nginx forwards /api/ requests to the backend.
- Frontend and backend ports are not exposed directly.
- Adding todos works successfully.
- Todos remain saved after refreshing the application.
- Direct access to localhost:3000 and localhost:5173 is refused.

The application is accessed through http://localhost:8080.
